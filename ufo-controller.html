      How It Works

      When enabled, the UFO polls the Dynatrace Problems API at the configured interval. Open problems are mapped to LED colours on the rings — typically red for active problems and green when everything is healthy. The UFO firmware stores these credentials in its local flash (SPIFFS) — they are not transmitted beyond your local network.
